This guide demonstrates how to evaluate whether an EC2 instance downsizing is viable by simulating EBS burst credit behavior. It shows how to pull CloudWatch metrics, compare workload patterns against target instance limits, and determine if smaller instances would experience throttling, with worked examples and guidance on post-migration monitoring.
