This article presents a solution template for deploying retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems on AWS Outposts and Local Zones. It describes a four-instance architecture combining vector embeddings, reranking, a vector database, and small language models to create on-premises AI chatbots that maintain data residency while providing advanced AI capabilities.
